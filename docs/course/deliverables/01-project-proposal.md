# Project proposal

### See the [grading criteria](../project/grading-criteria.md), they can help when designing your project app and get a common understanding in the group regarding what grade you aim for.

[Slides from the project preparation lecture](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1l6zjcnWJZib-DbMerVDL832DchAEyvP_MNDb1lHSQuw/edit#slide=id.g29e124abe43_0_96). Or watch the [video](https://play.kth.se/media/DH2642+VT24+lecture+7A+project+preparation/0_ujhvupij)

## Assignment

You may not do your project alone since **cooperation** is a course goal!

[As a group](../project/project-intro-and-grading.md) of max 4 you should come up with an idea for an interactive application that fulfils the grading criteria. [See previous years' projects](../project/hall-of-fame-examples.md) - your project should of course be original.

## What to submit

First, [be sure you have joined a project group](../project/project-intro-and-grading.md). By the deadline (specified below) you should, as a group, submit the project proposal consisting of:

- your names and Canvas IDs. It is important to confirm who worked in the group so the credit does not go to somebody else.
- name of the app
- short description
- framework(s) that will be used
- API that will be used
- what data will your app work with (which ones come from API and which one is your app specific)
- quick mock-up/prototype (paper drawing is fine) of the screens your app will have

The project proposal will be reviewed and approved by the teaching staff. The purpose of the approval is to ensure that the project is complex enough to fulfil the requirements but not too complex. You may e.g. be asked to add features. You don't need to wait for the proposal approval to start working on the project, but **be sure to implement** any feedback you receive in Canvas.

## After submission

Discuss your project proposal with your project coach!

When you start working on your idea, see [Project advice](../project/project-advice.md) on Canvas
