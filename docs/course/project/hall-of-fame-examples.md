Here are couple of very good projects from previous years. Some might not work fully, as API's or other dependencies might have changed.

We don't guarantee the projects from before 2020 would have gotten A grades now, since a new [grading matrix](grading-criteria.md) is in place. However, they are all brilliant examples of original, useful, and/or well-crafted applications, and serve very well as inspiration.

## Projects from <2020

## 2019

### [Junkfood Burner](https://junkburner-ed983.firebaseapp.com/#/)

Exercise compiler mixed with calories for meals. Robust, reasonably complex, and mashes several API's together.

### [BookBook](https://bookbook.tech/#/welcome)

Bookshelf tracker with barcode scanning functionality. Note that this functionality is perfect for the target group, which a big reason why this is such a good project.

### [NaviRad](https://navirad-d74c7.firebaseapp.com/)

Radar navigation simulator on an actual map you get to select. A unique idea, and the implementation is adapted to the user group (looks "marine", you navigate with a "joystick"...)

## Earlier
